    📖 How to play

    Take turns rolling one die and moving clockwise. Resolve the attraction or card where you land, then select End Turn.

    Earn tickets from successful attractions and challenges. Mystery Cards can add or subtract tickets; scores never fall below zero.

    Game length: 2 players play 10 rounds each; 3 players play 7; 4 players play 5. Most tickets wins. First-place ties go to a tiebreak roll.

    Only the active player can act. All dice results and scoring are managed by the game server.
